Privacy Policy

PopChats · Last updated: 1 July 2026

PopChats (“we”, “us”) is a social chat service. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. PopChats is for adults aged 18 and over only.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), and — if you use Google — your Google profile name and picture.
  • Profile: username, display name, date of birth (used only to verify you are 18+; not shown publicly), gender (optional), bio, and avatar.
  • Content: messages you send (stored encrypted at rest), and any images or video you choose to share.
  • Usage & technical: online status, call metadata (who/when/duration — not call audio/video), device/push tokens, and IP address (via our infrastructure and anti-abuse providers).

2. How we use it

  • To provide chat, matching, calls, and notifications.
  • To verify you are 18+ and keep the platform safe (moderation, anti-abuse, reporting).
  • To detect and block illegal content. Uploaded media is automatically scanned; child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is blocked and reported to the relevant authorities as required by law.

3. Service providers

We share data with processors strictly to run the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, storage.
  • Google — optional sign-in.
  • Cloudflare — anti-abuse / bot protection (processes IP addresses).
  • Automated content-moderation provider(s) for safety scanning.

4. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Messages and media are retained until you or the other participant delete them, or until your account is deleted. Safety and legal records may be retained longer where the law requires.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and content (subject to legal retention). To exercise these rights, contact us below. This policy honours the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, for applicable users, the GDPR.

6. Children

PopChats is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly allow minors. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we remove it.

7. Security

Messages are encrypted at rest and access is restricted by row-level security. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password.
